ℹ️ PokéScan Data Notes

Card data: English card searches use the public Pokémon TCG API to retrieve card names, sets, numbers, images, and some TCGplayer price data. Internet access is required, and the API may occasionally be unavailable.

Other language versions: Photo OCR is a text-reading aid, not Pokémon-card-specific AI recognition. This US-focused version does not automatically collect or verify prices for other language versions.

Price Source: Prices included in the API are labeled as provider data. External links are search links for you to check yourself. PokéScan does not present unverified prices as confirmed market values.

Card condition: A photo alone cannot reliably determine corners, surface, or centering. The checklist is for personal reference, not an official or professional grade.

Storage: Your collection and price history are saved in this browser. They may be lost if browser data is cleared and are not automatically shared across devices, so export backups regularly.
